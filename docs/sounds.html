Audio Bay — Sound Catalog

Every clip below streams straight from trekcore.com's public audio archive — nothing here is hosted or bundled by this repo. Preview a clip, then click "Use for…" to have your own local Tricorder fetch it into ~/.claude/tricorder/sounds/ and assign it to that event. This only works if Tricorder's menu-bar app is running on your Mac and registered for the tricorder:// URL scheme — otherwise the link does nothing.
